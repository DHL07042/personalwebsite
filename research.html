01

Foot⁠–⁠ground distance sensing

Time-of-flight (ToF) sensors measure foot⁠–⁠ground distance during walking. The distance signal remains available during swing, complementing pressure-based measurements during stance.

J1 · JKSPE 2026T1 · ISPS 2021M1 · in preparation

Real-time gait-phase detection

What I did

Designed an overshoe-type module with two ToF sensors, near the lateral heel and the fifth metatarsophalangeal (MTP) joint, and a lightweight threshold-based algorithm that segments gait phases in real time [J1]. A 2021 presentation covered full-phase detection with multi-modal sensor-integrated shoes [T1].

Evaluation

Tested with one healthy participant walking on a flat indoor floor. Compared gait-phase estimates with pressure-based measurements and recorded foot⁠–⁠ground distance during swing [J1].

Preparing an exoskeleton for ground contact

What I did

Designed and built a foot⁠–⁠ground distance module (custom PCB, four ToF sensors, STM32 microcontroller) and integrated it into the Angel Legs M20 exoskeleton. On top of it I implemented a layer that uses the distance signal to retune the joint’s virtual spring and damper before the foot lands, and ran walking experiments over tandem force plates with the layer off and at two settings. This was my M.S. thesis, Terminal Decision Maker Against Upcoming Surface for the Safe Locomotion of Wearable Robots.

Status

A manuscript, FDL: Foot⁠–⁠Ground Distance Layer for Initial-Contact Load Modulation in a Lower-Limb Exoskeleton, is in preparation [M1]. It builds on the thesis experiments.

02

Exoskeleton instrumentation and shock-absorption experiments

Measuring foot⁠–⁠ground and hand–crutch forces during exoskeleton walking, to evaluate a shock-absorption mechanism on the shank.

J2 · IJCAS 2023C1 · ICRA 2021J5 · ICROS 2020

What I did

Developed an FSR-based crutch-force module [J5] and pneumatic plantar-pressure modules, fabricated components and instrumented a modified WalkON Suit 4, and ran the shock-absorption experiments [C1, J2].

Reported result

In the journal study, the shock absorber with the designed gait pattern reduced the mean peak heel ground-reaction force by 23% compared with no absorber, in one participant [J2]. The journal article extends the ICRA 2021 study [C1].

03

WalkON Suit and CYBATHLON 2020

Hardware work on the team’s powered exoskeletons for the CYBATHLON 2020 Powered Exoskeleton Race.

J3 · IEEE IEM 2022

What I did

Assembled and debugged the team’s exoskeletons through the competition schedule, modified the foot module to fit a wider range of users, and integrated the pressure-sensing foot hardware used for human-intention detection.

Team outcome

Gold and bronze medals in the Powered Exoskeleton Race. Three graduate students on the team, including me, received the KAIST Global Leadership Award. The overview article [J3] describes the WalkON Suit system.

04

Experimental systems for wearable-robot evaluation

Measurement-system integration and analysis software for wearable-robot evaluation.

Instrumentation

Integrated VICON motion capture, an AMTI instrumented treadmill, Delsys EMG, Xsens inertial sensors, and custom sensors into one evaluation setup.

Analysis and modeling

Wrote the MATLAB analysis software and built human⁠–⁠robot models in OpenSim.
