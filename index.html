Daeho Lee

Researcher, Agency for Defense Development (ADD)Daejeon, South Korea

I work on sensing and control at the foot⁠–⁠ground and human⁠–⁠robot interfaces of wearable robots.

At KAIST’s Exo Lab, I worked with Prof. Kyoungchul Kong on sensor development and experimental evaluation of powered exoskeletons. My contributions included distance- and force‑sensing hardware, robot integration, and walking experiments. I received my B.S. (2020) and M.S. (2022) in mechanical engineering from KAIST.

My research interests include gait‑phase estimation, contact‑aware load modulation, and human‑intention recognition in wearable robots. I am also interested in sensing and control for legged locomotion.
