Daeho Lee

Researcher, Agency for Defense Development

Daejeon, South Korea · Since April 2023

My research background is in wearable robotics, gait-phase detection, and force measurement.

At KAIST’s Exo Lab, I developed sensor hardware, integrated measurement systems with lower-limb exoskeletons, and conducted experiments. I received my B.S. and M.S. in mechanical engineering from KAIST, advised during my master’s research by Prof. Kyoungchul Kong.

My research interests include foot–ground interaction, human–robot interaction forces, and sensing for the control of wearable and legged robots.
