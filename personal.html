Science outreach · 2021

Robotics education for children

The Nerd Lab · Daejeon Shinsegae Art & Science

Our team developed robotics learning materials for children as part of a collaboration with Daejeon Shinsegae Art & Science. A wearable demonstrator with an encoder and an inertial measurement unit helped introduce sensing and sensor fusion.

The project ran from April to August 2021.

Film and editing · 2016–2018

Video production at AFN Korea

During my military service as a KATUSA, I worked as a broadcaster and video editor at AFN Korea. I filmed and edited feature stories and clips that aired on the network.
