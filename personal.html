Beyond research

Outreach, video work, and what I do off duty.

Outreach · 2021

Robotics education for children

The Nerd Lab, Daejeon Shinsegae Art & Science · April–August 2021

For the opening of a new Shinsegae department store in Daejeon, our team developed robotics learning materials for children. To show how robots use sensors and how sensor fusion works, we built a wearable demonstrator with an encoder and an inertial measurement unit.

Video · 2016–2018

Video journalism at AFN Korea

Mandatory military service as a KATUSA · August 2016–April 2018

I served at the American Forces Network (AFN) Korea as a video journalist, filming and editing stories that aired across the Pacific region, and was discharged as a sergeant.

Off duty

Travel photography and noraebang

I take photographs when I travel, and I sing and rap at noraebang, the Korean karaoke room.
